<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-calendar-week:{ .feature-icon } **A plan for every weekday**

    Give Monday through Friday its own lessons, start time, durations, breaks, and meeting links. Your schedule survives restarts in a local JSON file.

-   :material-notebook-edit:{ .feature-icon } **Homework on your calendar**

    Add notes and photos to a weekday, browse homework week by week, and keep Gemini's step-by-step explanation beside it.

-   :material-palette:{ .feature-icon } **Make it yours**

    Switch between English and Ukrainian, choose light, dark, or system appearance, and pick an accent color.

-   :material-shield-lock:{ .feature-icon } **Private by default**

    Your timetable and class links stay on your computer. Gemini only receives homework when you explicitly ask it to solve a task.
