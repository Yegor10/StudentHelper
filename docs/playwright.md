# Scheduled meeting links

The desktop app and Chrome extension use your regular browser for scheduled lessons. One minute before a lesson with a meeting URL, StudentHelper opens that link in a new browser tab.

The app does not join the call, change microphone or camera settings, or bypass a waiting room. Check the browser tab and join when ready.

Keep StudentHelper running for scheduled links. If your computer is asleep at the scheduled time, the link opens after the scheduler resumes. Lessons without a URL generate a reminder instead.

This avoids a separate Playwright Chromium sign-in profile, so Google sign-in and saved browser sessions use your normal Chrome or default-browser profile.
