# Features

StudentHelper keeps the everyday study workflow together in a small desktop app. The interface and its feedback are available in English and Ukrainian.

## Plan a five-day week

Each weekday has its own first-class start time and lesson list. A lesson stores its title, duration, break after class, and optional online-meeting URL. The JSON schedule is retained after closing and reopening the app.

## Keep homework connected to the week

See your homework in a five-day calendar inspired by Google Calendar. Browse week by week, select a day, and add a text or photo task there. Notes and AI explanations remain in the local diary; tasks are not automatically duplicated from week to week.

## Work through a problem

Ask Gemini for a detailed explanation when you are ready. The image and prompt are sent to Google only when you press **Solve with AI**. The resulting solution is stored alongside the task.

## Choose a comfortable look

Select **System**, **Light**, or **Dark** appearance. Pick the blue, green, or midnight-blue accent. Language, appearance, and accent are remembered locally.

## Open scheduled meetings

When a saved meeting is near its scheduled start, StudentHelper opens it in a visible Playwright-controlled Chromium profile. Provider pages vary; see [meeting automation](playwright.md) for what is and is not automated.
